Controlling object: Orange portal
PC controls:
  • W, A, S, D to move
  • Space, shift to fly up and down
  • Mouse to look around, left / right click to shoot the orange / blue portal
  • P to change controlling object
  • I, K, J, L to move controlling object
  • U, O to move controlling object up and down
  • B, N, M to rotate controlling object around the X, Y, Z world axis
  • When looking at companion cube, E to pick it up
  • When holding the companion cube, E again to throw it
  • When holding the companion cube, F to let go of it
  • When not holding the companion cube, F to teleport it in front of you
  • G to toggle gravity for the companion cube
